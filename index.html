Spikeball Ref

Point your phone at the net. It follows the ball and flags rim hits, roll-ups and double bounces the moment they happen.

  1. Prop the phone 1–2 m from the net, about knee to waist high, so the rim looks like a flat oval.
  2. Turn the phone sideways and keep it still. A cheap tripod or a water bottle works.
  3. Drag the oval onto the rim, then play.
